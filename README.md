# HR Analytics Dashboard

This repository contains an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI**.  
The dashboard provides insights into employee attrition, demographics, job roles, salary slabs, and overall workforce analytics.

---

##  Dashboard KPIs

- **Total Employees:** 1470  
- **Attrition:** 237  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.1%  
- **Average Age:** 37  
- **Average Salary:** 6.5K  
- **Average Years at Company:** 7 years  

---

##  Visualizations Included

### **1. Attrition by Education**
Donut chart showing attrition distribution across:
- Life Sciences  
- Medical  
- Marketing  
- Technical Degree  
- Other  

### **2. Attrition by Age Group**
Bar chart displaying employee attrition in the following age ranges:
- 18–25  
- 26–35  
- 36–45  
- 46–55  
- 55+  

### **3. Gender-wise Attrition**
- Male  
- Female  

### **4. Attrition by Salary Slab**
Horizontal bar chart:
- Upto 5k  
- 5k–10k  
- 10k–15k  
- 15k+

### **5. Attrition by Years at Company**
Line chart showing attrition trend based on tenure.

### **6. Job Role vs Attrition**
Matrix table with job role and rating-wise attrition counts.

### **7. Attrition by Job Role**
Horizontal bar chart showing highest attrition among:
- Sales Executive  
- Research Scientist  
- Laboratory Technician  
- Sales Representative
